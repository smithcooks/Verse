# Verse desktop design — 0.1.4

User direction: neutral translucent controls, clean bold word typography, no pink theme, album-derived fullscreen atmosphere. UI/UX Pro Max glassmorphism guidance informs light borders, restrained reflections and accessible focus; its suggested purple/green palette and external fonts were rejected in favor of the user's direction and low-resource system fonts.

Tokens: charcoal #0c0d11 background, white #f4f2f6 text, cool white #dce4ed accent, white borders at 8–25% opacity. Segoe UI for chrome; lyric size 28–240px, default 112px/800. Rounded 12–24px panels and pill controls. Glass blur is 12px on small surfaces; sidebar and lyric preview have no backdrop blur. The artwork backdrop is downsampled/blurred once to 192px. No per-frame image rendering or full-resolution blur.

Motion: individual native glyphs, five visible words maximum, transform/opacity transition 220ms, active-word pop 230ms. Explicit seeks reset transitions; timing corrections preserve forward word order. Reduced motion removes transitions and background drift. No clock, colourful lyric boxes, or glass glyph shader.

Interaction: persistent player controls across tabs; fullscreen playback controls reveal on hover/focus; source selector, live size slider, font/weight/finish controls, keyboard colour wheel, brightness and standard colour swatches. Overlay keeps the desktop transparent; artwork/gradient/plain backgrounds apply to fullscreen.

0.1.5 Music library: Listen/History/Liked tabs, a now-playing artwork card, four-column radio album cards at laptop width, local listening shelves and a sticky Up next panel. Search is a toolbar control instead of the whole page. Glass cards use static highlights, light borders and restrained reflections; album images load lazily. Personalization is explicitly based on local recent listening rather than a signed-in account.
0.1.6 full-window material: desktop-visible transparent native window, translucent blue/charcoal/teal wash and clearer glass edges; opaque text/artwork. Custom draggable title bar replaces opaque OS chrome. Explicit Expand/Restore avoids unsupported transparent-window native resizing/maximizing. Fullscreen uses three static gradient textures with compositor transform/opacity motion (13–18s cycles), with artwork-derived colours and blue/teal/amber fallback. No animated blur or per-frame palette work. Gradient is the new default, including a one-time profile migration. Page transitions are 280ms and hover lifts 260ms; Reduced Motion also disables pseudo-element animations.
0.1.7 user refinement: smoked-white window wash at roughly 75–80% opacity replaces the overly transparent dark tint. Dark slate UI text maintains contrast; lyric preview/fullscreen retain white glyphs. Glass hover/focus reflection uses only transform/opacity; a slow ambient white/blue layer breathes behind every page. Reduced Motion disables both. No desktop blur or new dependency.
0.1.8 screenshot review: pale white wash rejected by the user. Rebalanced to smoky gray/slate at about 82–86% base opacity, keeping desktop translucency and using white only for restrained reflections. Bright text, softer panel borders and ambient/hover transform-opacity motion retained.
